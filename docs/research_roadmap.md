# Research Roadmap

This page tracks exploratory directions and candidate sources referenced by the HART-Morphosis blueprint.

## Explore the Foundations

### MIT’s HART Architecture

- **Focus**: Hybrid Autoregressive Transformer approaches for efficient, mobile-friendly image generation.
- **Suggested resources**:
  - https://arxiv.org/search/?query=autoregressive+image+transformer+efficient&searchtype=all
  - https://arxiv.org/search/?query=mobile+vision+transformer+inference&searchtype=all

### Neural Cosmic Web

- **Focus**: Quantitative comparisons between neural connectivity graphs and large-scale cosmic structure.
- **Suggested resources**:
  - https://arxiv.org/search/?query=cosmic+web+network+analysis&searchtype=all
  - https://arxiv.org/search/?query=brain+connectome+network+topology&searchtype=all

### Quantum Denoising

- **Focus**: Quantum-inspired denoising algorithms that reference Schrödinger-style formulations for signal filtering.
- **Suggested resources**:
  - https://arxiv.org/search/?query=schrodinger+equation+denoising&searchtype=all
  - https://arxiv.org/search/?query=quantum+inspired+image+denoising&searchtype=all
